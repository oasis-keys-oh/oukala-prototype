How resource planning works
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to Capacity and demand planning, Team Topologies.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
AUTOMATIC
Collect demand
Every work type reports hours per person per week: projects, incidents, requests, changes, on-call, operational work, meetings, training.
AUTOMATIC
Subtract leave and holidays
Approved time off and the company calendar reduce available hours. Contracts end on their end date.
AI AGENT
Spot pressure
Group and person load against the target band for the role family.
AI AGENT
Propose fixes
Move work, add a contractor, offload to an agent, or defer. Each with cost and risk.
APPROVAL GATE
Decide and request
A manager applies a fix or raises a resource request. The resource manager approves.
PERSON
Fill and re-plan
The filled request adds hours to the named person and the plan updates.
