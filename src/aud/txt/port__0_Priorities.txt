How demand becomes funded work
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to PMBOK, TBM Taxonomy and ATUM, SAFe.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
PERSON
Raise
Anyone fills the form, emails intake, uploads a contract, or a trigger creates the request: unit-cost scale up or down, service level breach, release finding, maturity gap.
AI AGENT
Screen
Portfolio Agent checks duplicates and missing facts, links the service, and suggests the four 0 to 5 ratings.
PERSON
Score and confirm
The sponsor confirms the ratings. Triggered requests wait here for a person.
AUTOMATIC
Rank
Scores, weights, the envelope and capacity set the order and the funding line. Manual moves are logged with a reason.
APPROVAL GATE
Review board
The board funds, parks or returns each request.
AUTOMATIC
Turn into a project
A funded request becomes a project with a plan, budget line, team and place on the master schedule.
