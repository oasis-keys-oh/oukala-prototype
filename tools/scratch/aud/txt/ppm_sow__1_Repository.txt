From vendor proposal to managed contract
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to WorldCC contracting practice, COBIT 2019, PMBOK.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
PERSON
Intake
A vendor proposal, SOW or terms arrive by upload, email or request. The original file and version are stored.
AI AGENT
AI analysis
The Contract Analyst Agent splits the text into typed lines with owners, dates, measures and confidence.
AI AGENT
Playbook match
Each line is matched to a standard clause, a variance with a redline, or flagged as new for legal.
PERSON
Legal or shared review
Legal reviews exceptions. Shared review adds the vendor and business owner in one thread with a deadline.
APPROVAL GATE
Approval
The signer is chosen by contract value under the delegation of authority. AI cannot accept terms.
AUTOMATIC
Execution
The executed contract is stored with its proposal. Project, WBS, invoice schedule and budget are created.
AI AGENT
Obligations tracking
Due dates, renewal notices and acceptance checks run daily. Invoices wait for accepted work.
APPROVAL GATE
Change control
Any change to schedule, scope, price, SLA or term is a change request with impact analysis and the right approver.
