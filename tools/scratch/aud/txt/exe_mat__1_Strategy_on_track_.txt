How a capability's level is set and how it moves
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to CMMI, ISO/IEC 33020, ITIL 5.

Choose or ingest a model
A model comes from the library, from a file the parser reads, or from the builder. Levels need criteria.
AI AGENT
AI maps capabilities
The Maturity Agent maps each capability to a strategic objective and the services that realize it, with a confidence. A person confirms.
PERSON
Assess today's level
The owner scores the level from evidence: linked metrics, reviews and audits.
APPROVAL GATE
Set targets
Level wanted at year 1, 3 and 5. Parents pass targets to children and prerequisites.
PERSON
Projects deliver
A project or request raises a capability from X to Y at a milestone.
APPROVAL GATE
Accept and credit
A named person accepts the work. Only then does the level count today.

Recalculate roll-ups
