Understand the ask
The planner scores every registered tool against the words, the page, the selected record and who the work is for. It uses rules, not a model, so the same ask gives the same plan.
AUTOMATIC
Check guardrails and permissions
Ten written policies and the role of the person acting are checked. If the chat acts for someone else, a delegation must exist.
PERSON
Show the plan
Steps, parameters, risk, cost and approvers appear as a card. The person can approve, edit or cancel.
APPROVAL GATE
Approvals from other people
Manager, budget owner or change advisory board steps wait as gates. The chat never approves its own plan for a person who is not allowed.
AI AGENT
Run step by step
Each tool runs against the real module, with live status. A failed step stops the plan and offers retry or handoff.

Audit and learn
Every step writes an audit entry with model, confidence and policy. Undo is offered where the tool supports it. Deflection and completion feed the metrics.
