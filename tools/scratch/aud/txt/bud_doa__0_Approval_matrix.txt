Where the invoice rules fit
The Invoice approval screen decides payment of an invoice against an approved order. This matrix decides who may commit the money in the first place.
An order approved at tier T3 can still be paid by rule r1 if the invoice is up to $25,000, the vendor index is at least 0.85 and it matches the order. Rule r4 escalates invoices above $100,000 to the CFO, the same line as the top of tier T3.
