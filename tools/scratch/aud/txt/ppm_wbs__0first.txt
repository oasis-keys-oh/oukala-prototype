How the work breakdown becomes the schedule
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to PMBOK, SAFe.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
PERSON
Decompose
The project manager breaks each deliverable into phases, work packages and tasks, and writes the WBS dictionary entry: deliverable and acceptance criteria.
AI AGENT
Draft the plan
PMO Agent suggests tasks, durations and links from similar past projects and from the statement of work. It drafts; a person accepts.
AUTOMATIC
Schedule
The schedule engine runs a forward and a backward pass over all four link types, lags and constraints, then rolls summaries up.
APPROVAL GATE
Approve the baseline
The steering committee approves the plan. Setting or resetting a baseline needs a reason and is audited.
PERSON
Track
Percent complete and actual dates arrive from task sheets. Variance, earned value and resource load update everywhere.
