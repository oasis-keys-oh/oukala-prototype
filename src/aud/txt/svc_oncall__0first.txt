How on-call cover is built and used
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to ITIL 5.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
PERSON
Define the rota
A team, service or asset class gets layers: first responder, primary, secondary, escalation. Providers and AI agents can hold layers.
AUTOMATIC
Check coverage
Required hours are compared with people scheduled and available, minus time off.
AI AGENT
Recommend and fill
The recommendation engine sizes shifts from SLA response time, incident volume and skills, and prices the options.
APPROVAL GATE
Approve changes
Leads approve swaps, overrides and time off that collide with a shift.
AUTOMATIC
Route and page
Incidents go to the team, then the on-call person is paged by the escalation policy.
PERSON
Hand over
The outgoing person writes a hand-over note; the next rotation starts.
