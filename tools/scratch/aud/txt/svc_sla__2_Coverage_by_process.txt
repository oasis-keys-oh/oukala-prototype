How an agreement is made, measured and kept
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to ITIL 5, Experience level agreements.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
PERSON
Define the agreement
The service level manager names what is promised, to whom, and for which services, CIs, priorities and customer tiers.
PERSON
Choose real fields
Conditions for start, pause, resume, stop, reset and cancel are built from fields in the platform data model, so the clock reads the same records everyone else does.
APPROVAL GATE
Preview and approve
The designer runs the draft over the last 30 days. The owner and the other party approve before the activation date.
AUTOMATIC
Measure
Each matching record gets a clock that follows the schedule, time zone and holidays. Pauses stop the clock.
AI AGENT
Warn and escalate
At 50%, 75% and 100% of the target the agreement notifies, pages or opens a bridge. The Triage Agent offers to hand the record to the best available responder.
PERSON
Review breaches
Breaches get a reason. The Pareto and recommendations show what to fix, and vendor credits are worked out from the contract clause.
APPROVAL GATE
Renegotiate or retire
Agreements that are always green get tighter, always red get reviewed, and obsolete ones get a retire date.
