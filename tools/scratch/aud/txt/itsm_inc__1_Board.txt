Incident process (ITIL 5 Support activity)
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to ITIL 5, ISO/IEC 20000.

AUTOMATIC
Detect and log
Monitoring, a caller or an agent raises the incident against a service and CI.
AUTOMATIC
Calculate priority
Impact is computed from the service's key metrics. Urgency is the customer's choice within the role bounds. Priority comes from the matrix.
AI AGENT
Confirm triage
An agent suggests category, team and similar incidents. A person confirms.
AUTOMATIC
Route to a team
Work lands in the team queue. Individuals are not assigned from outside the team.
PERSON
Claim and swarm
A team member claims it, or the team swarms. A direct assignment needs a stated reason.
PERSON
Diagnose and restore
Work the known error or runbook, link a change if one is needed, communicate.
AI AGENT
Resolve and learn
Record cause and code. An agent drafts the knowledge article and suggests a problem if it repeats.
