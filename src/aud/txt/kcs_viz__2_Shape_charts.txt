Which shape chart fits?
A short guide.
Shape fill or liquid: one number against a target, such as capacity used or a goal reached.
Stacked silhouette: a total split into shares, drawn as the thing itself.
Silhouette bars: category sizes where area is proportional to value, for example cars sold by model.
Pictogram and pictorial bars: counts people can picture; always say what one icon equals.
Person waffle: percentages of people, 100 icons.
Custom shape and image fill: brand-neutral shapes of your own; SVG is sanitized before use.
