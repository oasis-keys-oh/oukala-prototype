ƒ Article quality score
Quality = 0.35 × Completeness + 0.25 × Clarity + 0.15 × Citations + 0.25 × Freshness
Completeness
Points for each filled section: symptom 20, applies to 10, cause 20, resolution 35, notes 5, plus 10 for any related record or source.
Clarity
100 minus 6 for every word that the average sentence runs over 10, plus 4 when the resolution has three or more numbered steps.
Citations
30 points for each source or related record (up to two records), capped at 100.
Freshness
See the Freshness tab.
Worked example: KB0140 scores 0.35×90 + 0.25×100 + 0.15×0 + 0.25×0 = 57. Fix the lowest part first: review the article.
