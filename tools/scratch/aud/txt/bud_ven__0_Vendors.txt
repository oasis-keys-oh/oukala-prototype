How the scorecard is calculated
Four things, weighted. Cost penalizes change orders, billing ahead of accepted work, billing errors and rates above the benchmark.

ƒ Three-way match rate
Scorecard = 0.30 × Delivery + 0.30 × Quality + 0.20 × Cost + 0.20 × Responsiveness
Cost = max(0, 1 − 2 × change-order % − unearned billing % − 0.05 × billing errors − rate premium %)

Worked example, Lumen: 0.30 × 0.75 + 0.30 × 0.71 + 0.20 × 0.86 + 0.20 × 0.66 = 0.74. Cost is 0.86 because change orders add 5.2% and $22K has been paid ahead of accepted work.
