# Visual, UX and UI audit: SERVICE group (ITSM and catalog)

Build: `review_build/full.html` (91 screens). Reviewer: senior product designer pass, read-only on source.
Scope: itsm_home, itsm_inc, itsm_prb, itsm_chg, itsm_mi, itsm_rel, agile_board, agile_art, svc_360, svc_cat, svc_req, svc_portal, svc_asset, svc_sla, svc_oncall, svc_skills, svc_notify. (svc_360 and itsm_home open without view tabs; all other screens have 4 to 8 tabs.)

Method: every tab shot at 1440 light; every tab at 1000 light; first two tabs at 1440 dark, 400 phone (sample), and Enterprise scale (SCALE.set(true)); plus a DOM scan of every tab (font under 11px, clipped text, hit targets under 24px, equal-height empty cards). Screenshots are in `review_build/vs/` (naming: `<width><l|d>_<screen>_<tabIndex>.png`, `S_` prefix = Enterprise scale, `I_` = interaction shots). Tabs not individually opened by eye (checked by DOM scan and contact skim only): itsm_mi 0/2/3/4, itsm_rel 1/3/4, agile_board 2 to 7, agile_art 0/2 to 4, svc_cat 2/3, svc_req 1 to 3, svc_asset 1/2, svc_sla 3 to 5, svc_oncall 2 to 5, svc_skills 2 to 6, svc_notify 1 to 4.
Confidence in findings below: about 85% (seen directly). Items marked (DOM) come from the scan only, about 65%.

## Summary

Counts: blocker 1, major 22, minor 33, polish 7 (63 findings).

Overall: the visual language is strong (palette, glass, serif headings, avatars, legends in most charts). The problems are structural and repeat across screens, so fix the core ones first: (1) hero eats the first viewport, (2) tables clip their last column with no scroll cue, (3) the floating Ask Oukala button covers content in every right-hand column, (4) stretched equal-height cards leave large voids, (5) the process strip is cut off on every screen, (6) several charts and maps are not legible at real density (Gantt, fishbone, impact map, coverage matrix, catalog).
