Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to CMMI, ISO/IEC 33020, ITIL 5.

The Maturity Agent maps each capability to a strategic objective and the services that realize it, with a confidence. A person confirms.
