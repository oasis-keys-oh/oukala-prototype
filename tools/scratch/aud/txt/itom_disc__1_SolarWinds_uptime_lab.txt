Every node answers; availability moves by hundredths. Oukala asks Orion for 30 day availability, matches each node to a CI, writes the uptime onto the CI and re-computes the availability of every service above it. This sandbox sends a sample payload shaped like a SolarWinds Information Service (SWIS) query result.

1. Query sent to Orion
Healthy
SELECT n.NodeID, n.Caption, n.IPAddress, n.Status, n.StatusDescription, n.LastBoot, n.MachineType, n.Vendor,
       AVG(r.Availability) AS Availability30d, AVG(r.AvgResponseTime) AS AvgResponseTime, AVG(r.PercentLoss) AS PercentLoss
FROM Orion.Nodes n
JOIN Orion.ResponseTimeHistory r ON r.NodeID = n.NodeID
WHERE r.DateTime > ADDDAY(-30, GETUTCDATE())
GROUP BY n.NodeID, n.Caption, n.IPAddress, n.Status, n.StatusDescription, n.LastBoot, n.MachineType, n.Vendor
Endpoint
https://orion.atlantic.example:17774/SolarWinds/InformationService/v3/Json
Modules
NPM, SAM
Matching
IPAddress first, then Caption. Both are identifiers for class Computer.
Precedence
Orion owns uptime_30d and operational_status.
