Format guide

CSV: two blocks separated by a blank line. First block header level,name,criteria. Second block header capability,parent,current,year1,year3,year5,owner,description. Lines starting with # model: name it.

JSON: {"name","levels":[{"name","criteria"}],"capabilities":[{"name","parent","current","year1","year3","year5"}]}

Markdown: # Name, a ## Levels section with ### Level 3: Defined headings and bullets as criteria, then ## Capabilities bullets such as - Name (current 2, target 4, year 3 5). Indent a bullet to make it a sub capability.

Missing targets are filled in: Year 1 is one level up, later years keep climbing. The parser warns about anything it guessed.
