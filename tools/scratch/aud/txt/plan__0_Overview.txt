How a project plan is built and kept current
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to PMBOK.
Add a step
Open in Workflow designer
PERSON
Scope and WBS
The project manager breaks the scope into phases, deliverables and work packages. Every line gets a deliverable and an owner.
PERSON
Estimate and link
Durations, resources and predecessors are set. The engine shows the critical path as you work.
AI AGENT
AI completeness check
PMO Agent drafts missing deliverables, flags lines with no owner, no resource or no predecessor, and suggests fixes.
APPROVAL GATE
Approve and baseline
The sponsor approves the plan and a baseline is set. Variance is measured from here.
AUTOMATIC
Track and replan
Actuals flow in, the engine reforecasts, and changes to dates need a reason.
