Only a person with the override right can change the result, and a P1 downgrade needs a second approver.

Who may choose what
Urgency bounds by role. A request above the bound is lowered and the note says why.
ROLE	MOST URGENT ALLOWED	MAY CALL CRITICAL	MAY OVERRIDE PRIORITY	NOTES
Caller or employee	Medium	Only if a lead agrees	No	Sets urgency from their own view. Medium at most; the desk adjusts.
Service desk analyst	High	Only if a lead agrees	No	Can choose High. Critical needs a lead or incident manager to agree.
Team lead or service desk lead	Critical	Yes, impact Extensive or Significant	No	Can choose Critical when impact is Extensive or Significant.
Incident manager	Critical	Yes, impact Extensive or Significant	Yes, with a reason	Can choose Critical and override a calculated priority with a reason. A P1 downgrade needs a second approver.
Service owner	Critical	Yes, impact Extensive or Significant	Yes, with a reason	Can override for their own services, with a reason.
Executive (CIO or delegate)	Critical	Yes, impact Extensive or Significant	Yes, with a reason	Can override any priority. Every override is audited and reported monthly.
VIP levels
A VIP raises urgency by documented steps, never above High on its own, with an expiry and an owner.

Level 2: Executive team	1	P3 or higher	Mar 31	Executives run time-boxed external commitments. Approved by the CIO; reviewed every 6 months. Owner: Omar Hamid.
Level 3: Critical role in a time-boxed window	1	P3 or higher	Nov 20	Chief Editor and senior editors during the Codification beta publishing windows (PR1). Expires when the window closes. Owner: Maya Ortiz.
Incident process (ITIL 5 Support activity)
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to ITIL 5, ISO/IEC 20000.

Detect and log

AUTOMATIC
Calculate priority
Impact is computed from the service's key metrics. Urgency is the customer's choice within the role bounds. Priority comes from the matrix.
AI AGENT
Confirm triage

AUTOMATIC
Route to a team
Work lands in the team queue. Individuals are not assigned from outside the team.
PERSON
Claim and swarm
A team member claims it, or the team swarms. A direct assignment needs a stated reason.
PERSON
Diagnose and restore
Work the known error or runbook, link a change if one is needed, communicate.
AI AGENT
Resolve and learn
Record cause and code. An agent drafts the knowledge article and suggests a problem if it repeats.
