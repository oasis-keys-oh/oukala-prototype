How a risk is handled
Each step is a person, an AI agent, an automatic action or an approval gate. Select a step to see who owns it and how fast it should be. Aligned to PMBOK, ISO/IEC 27001.

Identify
Anyone or any agent logs a risk, assumption, issue, dependency or decision.
PERSON
Rate
Probability and impact are rated 1 to 5; risks also get a cost, so exposure is in dollars.
APPROVAL GATE
Own
Every item has an owner who can be an employee, an AI agent, a contractor or a provider. An AI agent needs an accountable human.
PERSON
Respond
Mitigate, accept, transfer or avoid. The response links to a schedule task.
AUTOMATIC
Watch exposure
Exposure adds up by project and owner kind; overdue and unowned items are flagged.
APPROVAL GATE
Close or escalate
A risk that happens becomes an issue; a closed item keeps its history.
